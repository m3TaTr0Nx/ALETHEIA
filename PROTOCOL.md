# Aletheia reusable protocol chassis

Aletheia treats every source body, interpretation, analogy, scientific lens, theological system, and generated narrative as a separately addressable object.

## State sequence

1. **INGEST** — full corpus, selected chapter, highlight, fragment, number sequence, mixed structured text, or arbitrary text.
2. **PROVENANCE LOCK** — source identity, edition/version, rights note, hash, and parent lineage where known.
3. **SEGMENT** — book/chapter/section/span boundaries while preserving exact source.
4. **TERMINOLOGY SWEEP** — exact surface forms, normalized lexemes, capitalization/case, aliases, contextual concept candidates, and ambiguity branches.
5. **FINITE-FIELD CARRIER** — deterministic term/concept coordinates for algebraic addressing and composition. Carrier collision never implies semantic identity.
6. **SOURCE GRAPH / HYPERGRAPH** — only relations supported by the source.
7. **SHAPE SUBSTRATE** — local structural signature plus optional Wolfram canonical hypergraph, isomorphism, embeddings, and invariants.
8. **MULTIWAY RECONCILIATION** — ambiguous terminology and ontology become branching candidate rewrites, never forced merges.
9. **LENS FABRICS** — independent philosophical, logical, theological, scientific, AI, engineering, biological, political, metaphysical, semiotic, or user-defined graphs.
10. **CONNECTORS** — typed cross-container relations such as lexical overlap, referent continuity, functional parallel, structural analogy, inverse parallel, contrast, non-equivalence, or unresolved.
11. **CUSTOM SUBSTITUTION FABRIC** — user mappings remain scoped derived objects.
12. **AI CONSTRUCTIVE PROPOSER** — models may propose graph edges, concepts, explanations, analogies, narratives, gaps, or storyboards; they may not edit the source object.
13. **ANALOGY FABRIC** — coherent written synthesis built from selected connectors and gate settings.
14. **STORYBOARD / VISUAL** — source-linked scenes and parallel adjacencies.
15. **EXPORT** — portable Fabric Bundle with provenance, graphs, confidence, model lineage, and firewall state.
16. **BUILD BRIDGES** — DiazAI, GrandState, Ultracode, and minting remain firewalled until explicit provenance and merge-integrity authorization.

## Non-collapse rule

Term equivalence is not concept equivalence. Concept equivalence is not ontology equivalence. Graph-shape similarity is not historical identity.

A modern graph may complete itself according to its own logic. It may not add missing nodes to a source graph simply to improve the match.
